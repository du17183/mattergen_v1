# Search-framework figure contract and legends

Backend: Python/Matplotlib only (saved nature-figure preference). Output: three independent single-panel vector PDFs and SVGs, 600-dpi PNG previews, approximately 89-mm nominal canvas width; final PDF bounding box may differ slightly due tight bounding. Source: `experiments/trajectory_search/finalize_evidence.py`; source-data CSVs in `results/trajectory_search/`. The figures use **only** completed fresh experimental data, no model-generated imagery or synthetic plotted observations.

## Compute-performance curve

Core conclusion: more frozen nested suffix candidates lower the **selected CHGNet proxy** error on this cohort, with nonuniform marginal yield. Results-level question: how does the observed K0–4 mean error vary with actual MatterGen score-call multiples? Archetype: single-panel quantitative dose–response curve; the K points are method states, not interpolated experimental budgets. Source: `results/trajectory_search/budget_scaling/{budget_scaling_summary.csv,mean_mae_bootstrap_20k.csv}`. Each point is a mean over the **same 128 paired fresh seeds**; vertical error bars are **20,000-resample bootstrap 95% CIs of the mean**, not paired-gain CIs. Score-call axis excludes verifier calls and wall time. No test/multiplicity adjustment is attached to the plotted marginal means. The line connects discrete points for readability, not a fitted scaling law. Reviewer risk: nested SAFE-A makes selected proxy MAE nonincreasing by construction; do not infer a universal smooth scaling effect or that K2 is optimal.

## Search-width curve

Core conclusion: incremental candidate value is highly uneven—near-zero at K3 but positive again at K4. Results-level question: what did **each newly added frozen suffix** contribute relative to the immediately preceding width on the same seeds? Archetype: single-panel paired-effect interval plot, distinct from the absolute compute-performance curve. Source: `results/trajectory_search/budget_scaling/incremental_paired_gains.csv`. Points are mean `MAE(K−1)−MAE(K)` for 128 paired seeds; bars are **post hoc descriptive 20,000-resample paired percentile 95% CIs**, no multiple-comparison correction, no null-hypothesis claim. The branch order is predeclared `GPulse, PPulse, APulse, CPulse`, so the K axis is ordered action-bank addition, not interchangeable width alone. K3 changed one seed; a zero lower bootstrap bound is expected for a near-all-zero paired distribution. Reviewer risk: do not relabel this as a preregistered primary test or use the K4 observation to retrofit C1.

## Method comparison

Core conclusion: an independently sampled Best-of-2 has numerically lower proxy MAE than Fixed-K2 at lower generator cost, with no conclusive direct paired difference. Results-level question: does the shared-prefix method clearly exceed a serious independent-sampling baseline? Archetype: single-panel method estimate with CI and visible budget labels. Source: `results/trajectory_search/best_of_2/{best_of_2_summary.csv,mean_mae_bootstrap_20k.csv}`. Each marker is a mean over the same 128 paired primary seeds; bars are **20,000-resample bootstrap 95% CIs of method means**, not a test of the paired contrast. The direct **paired** Best-of-2-minus-Fixed-K2 difference is `−0.001410`, 95% CI `[−0.005039,+0.002167]`; W/T/L `41/59/28`. Report this in the manuscript text/caption; overlapping marginal intervals alone are not its evidence. Budget labels are score-call multiples only: 1.0×/2.0×/2.2×. Reviewer risk: Fixed-K2 has 3 terminals versus Best-of-2's 2 and 10% more score calls; shared verifier and endpoint risk selection optimism.

For all figures: target is `dft_mag_density=0.1`; Property MAE is the CHGNet absolute property-error proxy. The unit is the model target's property unit; do not substitute “DFT MAE.” There is one stochastic draw per registered seed per policy, no train/validation split for these frozen inference comparisons, and no biological replicates. The statistical unit is the seed. Full numerical quality metrics and quality guardrails are in the CSVs/reports, not overplotted on the primary-error figures. No color encodes quality or significance.

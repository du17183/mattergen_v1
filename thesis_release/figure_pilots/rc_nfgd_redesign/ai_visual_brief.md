# RC-NFGD AI schematic: internal visual brief

Status: internal design draft; not a thesis or journal submission asset. AI-use
eligibility and disclosure requirements for the eventual venue remain unverified.

## Communication contract

- Audience: materials informatics and machine-learning readers.
- One-sentence message: an external neural-force signal enters only a gated
  late-stage position predictor; if validation fails, the original MatterGen
  trajectory continues unchanged.
- Figure type: method/process schematic, read left to right with one subordinate
  feedback loop. No quantitative result or performance claim.
- Required scientific entities: periodic predicted-clean crystal; neural
  interatomic potential as a frozen evaluator; centered Cartesian atom-force
  directions; small bounded displacement; position-only predictor insertion;
  the untouched atomic/cell fields and corrector; a safe bypass.
- Evidence boundary: the drawn crystal is generic and illustrative; no DFT,
  measured forces, improvement percentages, physical-stability claim, or named
  chemical composition may be implied.
- Omit: logos, badges, photographs, glow effects, gradients, 3-D scientific
  realism, chart axes, decorative molecules, invented data, manuscript text.

## AI role and human verification

AI creates two alternative visual compositions without text, numbers, symbols,
formulas, or arrows. All scientifically operative connections, captions and
formulas must be drawn later as deterministic editable vectors and checked
against the frozen method. The images are visual-concept candidates only.

## Prompt sent to image generation

Create a high-end scientific method schematic visual concept for a materials
machine-learning thesis, landscape 16:9, clean white background, flat vector-like
editorial illustration. This is one of two layout alternatives, not final art.
Visual vocabulary: thin precise charcoal strokes, muted cobalt blue for the
late-stage predictor, muted teal for an external frozen force evaluator, very
subtle warm amber for a safe bypass, ample white space, consistent line weight,
geometric rather than decorative shapes. Draw a left-to-right primary reverse-
diffusion path as a thin sequence of generic periodic crystal states, with only
the final short segment highlighted. Below that segment, show an explanatory
loop with (i) a simple oblique periodic unit cell holding a few generic atoms,
(ii) a compact abstract neural interatomic-potential evaluator, and (iii)
centered local force-vector cues and a small bounded geometry displacement.
Reserve clear empty corridors between these elements and a clear place for the
loop to rejoin the primary path. Reserve unobstructed whitespace for later
editable labels and equations. The result should look like a restrained
scientific journal schematic, not a slide template or technology infographic.
Absolutely no text, letters, numerals, equations, arrows, legends, logos,
watermarks, charts, real materials, or measurement marks. Do not claim or depict
stability improvement, DFT, or a specific crystal structure. Crisp edges.

\begin{ChineseAbstract}

材料逆向设计旨在根据目标性能或约束条件反推出满足要求的候选晶体结构。生成式深度学习为在大规模结构空间中直接产生候选提供了新的技术路径，但条件属性控制精度有限，且满足属性条件的结构仍可能存在局部几何或代理受力问题。本文以冻结的 MatterGen 作为基础生成模型，围绕推理阶段的条件属性控制和局部结构代理物理一致性开展研究，不重新训练 MatterGen 主干权重，也不改变其三字段生成定义。

据此，本文以“冻结材料生成模型的可信推理增强”为统一主线；“可信”特指推理预算、回退路径及代理证据边界可审计，不表示结构已通过 DFT 或实验物理验证。


针对冻结生成模型如何利用有限额外推理计算、又避免不利候选破坏参考输出的问题，本文提出预算约束验证选择推理方法。该方法保持 MatterGen 参数冻结，保存固定 CFG=2.0 的精确 C0 参考轨迹与共享采样前缀，按预定预算扩展候选后缀，并通过终点属性误差和代理质量约束选择输出；无合格候选时回退到 C0。Fixed-K2 是该框架的固定双候选实例。在 128 个全新配对种子组成的 C1-128 确认实验中，其 Property MAE 从 0.034796 降至 0.026332，相对降低 24.32\%，通过预定义代理质量护栏，MatterGen 得分调用约为 C0 的 2.2 倍。另一个独立的 128 种子对照中，独立 Best-of-2 的 Property MAE 为 0.028097，Fixed-K2 为 0.029506；两者的直接配对差异区间跨零，且预算并不完全相同。该结果表明额外采样计算本身具有竞争力，本文不主张 Fixed-K2 优于一般 Best-of-N，而关注参考保持、约束选择与可回退的推理机制。学习型 Linear-K2 分配亦未得到独立支持。

针对属性满足但局部结构仍可能具有较高代理受力的问题，本文提出基于阶段可靠性校准的后期神经力场引导扩散方法（Reliability-Calibrated Late-stage Neural Force-Guided Diffusion，RC-NFGD）。该方法依据预测干净结构在扩散后期的阶段可靠性构造介入窗口，调用 MatterSim 估计原子力，经笛卡尔坐标到周期分数坐标的映射后，将力反馈注入 position predictor，并实施有界位置修正和异常安全回退。在 Formal256 正式实验中，MatterSim 评估的最大原子力、平均原子力和 RMSD 分别降低 29.81\%、29.72\% 和 14.17\%；未参与在线引导的 CHGNet 独立机器学习原子间势评估中，最大原子力均值降低 14.36\%。与此同时，RC-NFGD 的 Property MAE 由 0.009757 增至 0.009868，误差约恶化 1.14\%，表明代理受力改善并未带来目标属性误差的同步改善。

补充性证据进一步限定方法边界：历史 A6 同组 32 对结构的属性---受力 Pareto 分析表明，RC-NFGD 与 POST 的方法均值呈不同折中，但 POST 的 MatterSim MaxF 更低，属性差异的配对区间跨零。另一个全新 32 对种子的力反馈注入阶段消融中，C0、Early、Middle、Late 的 MaxF 均值分别为 0.18986、0.16860、0.14072、0.13864 eV/\AA；原始 Late 相对 C0 的配对改善得到支持，但与 Early、Middle 的直接比较区间均跨零，且各阶段力调用预算不同，不能断言后期注入普遍最优。


综上，本文形成两条独立的冻结 MatterGen 推理增强路线：预算约束验证选择面向代理属性控制，RC-NFGD 面向代理局部结构一致性。预算扩展实验显示，在另一个独立 cohort 中，K=0/1/2/3/4 的 Property MAE 分别为 0.035141/0.031636/0.028454/0.028419/0.024669，说明 K=2 是既有成本折中实例而非最优搜索宽度。两条路线尚无已验证的联合增益；在线力引导也未全面优于等预算生成后处理。物理一致性证据仍来自 MatterSim 和 CHGNet 等机器学习原子间势代理，尚无 DFT 验证。结论因此限定于所用模型、任务、预算和代理协议。

\end{ChineseAbstract}

\begin{EnglishAbstract}

Inverse materials design aims to infer candidate crystal structures from target properties or structural constraints. Deep generative models provide a way to propose candidates directly in a large structural space, yet conditional property control can remain inaccurate, and a structure that satisfies a target property may still exhibit poor local geometry or large surrogate forces. This thesis uses frozen MatterGen as the foundation generative model and studies two inference-time problems: conditional property control and local structural consistency as measured by surrogate physical metrics. The MatterGen backbone and its three-field generation definition are kept unchanged.

Accordingly, the unifying theme is trustworthy inference-time enhancement of frozen material generative models. Here, trustworthy means auditable compute budgets, fallback paths, and surrogate-evidence boundaries; it does not imply DFT- or experimentally verified physical stability.


To study how a frozen generator can use limited additional inference compute without losing a reproducible reference output, this thesis proposes a budget-constrained verifier-guided inference framework. It preserves the exact CFG=2.0 C0 trajectory and a shared prefix, expands a preregistered set of candidate suffixes, and uses terminal property error and surrogate-quality constraints for selection, falling back to C0 when no candidate qualifies. Fixed-K2 is a fixed-width instance. On 128 fresh paired C1 seeds, it reduced Property MAE from 0.034796 to 0.026332 (24.32\%) while passing predefined surrogate-quality guardrails, at approximately 2.2 times the C0 generator score-call budget. In a separate 128-seed comparison, Independent Best-of-2 and Fixed-K2 achieved MAE values of 0.028097 and 0.029506, respectively; their direct paired confidence interval spans zero, and their compute and terminal-candidate budgets differ. Additional sampling itself is therefore competitive. This thesis does not claim that Fixed-K2 outperforms general Best-of-N sampling; its focus is reference preservation, constraint-aware selection, and fallback. Learned Linear-K2 allocation showed no independently supported added value.

To address the possibility that property-satisfying structures still have large local surrogate forces, this thesis proposes Reliability-Calibrated Late-stage Neural Force-Guided Diffusion (RC-NFGD). The method identifies an intervention window from the stage-wise reliability of predicted clean structures, evaluates atomic forces with MatterSim in the late diffusion stage, maps Cartesian forces to periodic fractional coordinates, injects the feedback into the position predictor, and applies bounded position correction with anomalous-path fallback. On Formal256, RC-NFGD reduced the MatterSim-evaluated maximum atomic force, mean atomic force, and RMSD by 29.81\%, 29.72\%, and 14.17\%, respectively. In an independent CHGNet machine-learning interatomic-potential evaluation that was not used for online guidance, the mean maximum atomic force decreased by 14.36\%. However, Property MAE increased from 0.009757 to 0.009868, corresponding to an approximately 1.14\% deterioration; thus, improved surrogate force and structural metrics did not translate into simultaneous improvement in target-property error.

Supplementary analyses delimit the physical-feedback claim. On the historical paired A6 cohort ($n=32$), the property--force Pareto analysis shows a descriptive trade-off between RC-NFGD and post-generation optimization (POST); POST has lower MatterSim MaxF, while the paired property-error interval spans zero. In a separate fresh 32-pair timing ablation, mean MaxF values for C0, Early, Middle, and the original Late intervention are 0.18986, 0.16860, 0.14072, and 0.13864 eV/\AA. Late improves on its paired C0 baseline, but direct Late--Early and Late--Middle intervals span zero, and force-call budgets differ. These results do not establish a universally optimal injection stage.


Overall, the thesis develops two independent inference-time enhancement routes for frozen MatterGen: budget-constrained verifier-guided selection for surrogate property control, and RC-NFGD for surrogate local structural consistency. In a separate budget-scaling cohort, Property MAE at widths K=0/1/2/3/4 was 0.035141/0.031636/0.028454/0.028419/0.024669; K=2 is the previously validated cost trade-off, not the best-performing width. The results do not establish joint synergy, nor does online force guidance dominate all equal-budget post-generation processing. Physical-consistency evidence remains limited to machine-learning interatomic-potential surrogates such as MatterSim and CHGNet; no DFT validation has been performed. Conclusions are restricted to the evaluated model, tasks, budgets, and surrogate protocols.

\EnglishKeywords{inverse materials design; crystal structure generation; diffusion models; budget-constrained inference; machine-learning interatomic potentials}
\end{EnglishAbstract}
